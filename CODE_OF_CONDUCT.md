# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Technical disagreement is welcome;
harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and repeated personal attacks are
not. Keep discussions relevant and constructive. Respect privacy and do not
publish another person's credentials or personal information.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions or
restrict participation. Raise moderation concerns with the repository maintainer
through an available private channel. If none is listed, ask for one without
posting private details publicly.
